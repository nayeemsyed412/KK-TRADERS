BROCHURE TECHNICAL RANGE

Specifications from the KK Steels brochure.

The website now carries the product names, component terminology and technical details presented in the supplied brochure.

01

Tie Rod & Frame Systems

Tie Rod: Cold rolled tie rod system.

Frames: Walk Thru, Ladder and other frames are available in all specification. The brochure describes sturdy welded frames with two verticals and two horizontals, connected by scissor cross braces through welded pins and locked by spring clips / gravity lock pin.

Cup-Lock System: Cup-lock components with forged blade ends and minimum projection to avoid damage. Each component locates in cup-lock joints on the vertical.

02

Horizontals & Verticals

Horizontals have identical forged blade ends with minimum projection. The brochure lists horizontal sizes of 1m width, 2m and 3m lengths, with 2 ledger blades attached; feet sizes are 3.3ft width, 4ft width, 6.6ft and 10ft lengths.

Pipe size: 40mm dia inner / 50mm outer dia, pipe thickness 2.8mm, with the brochure noting a 20ft long pipe and 20kg weight.

03

Junior Props & Threaded Components

Junior Props: made from 40NB outer and 33 OD inner pipe as IS standards, designed for beam support.

Threaded Sleeve: made from 50NB medium tube as per IS standards with heavy-duty malleable cast iron nut.

Roll Threaded Outers: rolled threading presses excess material into the pipe, preventing wastage and increasing load-taking capacity; made from 50NB special-grade tubes as per IS standards.

04

Prop Components

The brochure illustrates and names: Prop, Junior Prop, L-Support, Thread Sleeve, Standard Head, Beam Bottom, Stirrup Head, Base Plate, Cup Lock Standard, Cup Lock Ledger, and Fixed / Adjustable Jacks.

05

Cup-Lock Standard Sizes

3m vertical: standard cup lock every half metre, 6 cup locks attached for 3m height.

10ft vertical: standard cup lock every 20 inches, 6 cup locks attached for 10ft height (6 top cups, 6 bottom cups attached).

2m vertical: standard cup lock every half metre, 4 cup locks attached for 2m height.

6.6ft vertical: standard cup lock every 20 inches, 4 cup locks attached for 6.6ft height (4 top cups, 4 bottom cups attached).

06

Centering & Wall Form System

Centering plates: angle sizes listed as 25×25×3mm, 25×25×5mm and 32×32×3mm; sheet sizes 16 gauge (1.6mm), 14 gauge (2mm), 12 gauge (2.4mm), with any measurement / any size noted.

Conventional wall form shuttering: steel system using 12 gauge MS sheets with slotted 45×45mm angle/MS flat frame and stiffener, channel or heavy-duty soldiers/tubes/channel wales, assembled with four types of uniquely designed clips. Soldiers are supplied in 2.5m and panel height 1.25m; special 3.5m and 5m soldiers are also noted for high-pour concreting.

07

Column Boxes

The brochure lists angle sizes of 32×32×3mm, 35×35×5mm and 40×40×5mm, with sheet sizes 14 gauge (2mm), 12 gauge (2.4mm) and 10 gauge (3mm), and notes availability in any measurement / any size.

08

Acrow / Telescopic Span

Adjustable Acrow / Telescopic Span with roll-thread adjustable steel props. The brochure states tubes conform to medium-class outer specifications and shows heavy-duty cast iron malleable nuts. Strip rup head, flat plates or beam head are available; beam head is a separate attachment.
